# Employee Handbook

**Company:** Nexora Technologies  
**Document Type:** Employee Handbook  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026

## 1. Introduction

Welcome to Nexora Technologies!

This handbook provides employees with an overview of the company's workplace policies, employment practices, working arrangements, and employee benefits.

All employees are expected to read and follow the policies described in this handbook.

## 2. Working Hours

- The standard working week consists of five working days, Monday to Friday.
- Standard working hours are from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM.
- Employees are entitled to a one-hour lunch break.
- Employees may be required to work additional hours depending on project requirements and business needs.
- Any changes to an employee's regular working schedule must be approved by their reporting manager.

## 3. Attendance and Punctuality

- Employees are expected to report to work on time.
- Attendance must be recorded using the company's designated attendance system.
- Employees who expect to arrive late must inform their reporting manager as early as possible.
- Repeated unapproved late arrivals or unexplained absences may result in disciplinary action.
- Employees must notify their manager if they are unable to attend work due to an unexpected situation.

Detailed attendance and leave rules are described in the Leave and Attendance Policy.

## 4. Probation Period

- Newly hired employees must complete a probation period of six months.
- During probation, employee performance, conduct, technical skills, and overall suitability for the role will be evaluated.
- The company may extend the probation period if additional assessment time is required.
- Successful completion of probation will be communicated through an official confirmation letter.
- Employees on probation are subject to the company's applicable employment policies.

## 5. Employee Benefits

Eligible employees may receive the following benefits, subject to company policy and applicable employment terms:

- Health insurance coverage.
- Access to company-provided learning and development resources.
- Paid leave according to the Leave and Attendance Policy.
- Reimbursement of eligible business-related expenses according to the Expense Reimbursement Policy.

Specific eligibility requirements and benefit limits will be communicated by the Human Resources department.

## 6. Code of Conduct

All employees are expected to:

- Treat colleagues, customers, and business partners with respect.
- Maintain professional behavior in the workplace.
- Protect confidential company and customer information.
- Use company resources responsibly.
- Avoid conflicts of interest.
- Follow applicable security and data protection guidelines.

## 7. Information Security and Confidentiality

- Employees must not share confidential company information with unauthorized individuals.
- Company credentials must not be shared with other people.
- Employees must follow the company's information security policies.
- Any suspected security incident or unauthorized access must be reported to the IT or security team immediately.

## 8. Performance Reviews

- Employee performance may be reviewed periodically.
- Performance reviews may consider work quality, productivity, collaboration, communication, and achievement of assigned objectives.
- Managers may provide feedback and identify areas for improvement.
- The company may use performance review outcomes when making decisions about career development, compensation, and promotions.

## 9. Policy Compliance

All employees are responsible for understanding and following company policies.

The company reserves the right to update its policies when required. Employees will be informed of significant policy changes through official communication channels.

For questions regarding employment policies, employees may contact the Human Resources department.
