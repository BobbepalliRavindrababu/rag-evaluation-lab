# Leave and Attendance Policy

**Company:** Nexora Technologies  
**Document Type:** Leave and Attendance Policy  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026

## 1. Purpose

This policy explains the leave entitlements, attendance requirements, leave approval process, and rules applicable to employees of Nexora Technologies.

## 2. Leave Year

- The company's leave year runs from January 1 to December 31.
- Employees joining during the year receive leave entitlements on a prorated basis.
- Leave balances are maintained in the company's designated HR system.

## 3. Annual Leave

- Eligible employees receive 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
- Annual leave is intended for vacations, personal commitments, and planned time away from work.
- Employees should submit planned annual leave requests at least 3 working days in advance.
- Annual leave requires approval from the employee's reporting manager.
- Employees should check their available leave balance before submitting a request.

### 3.1 Annual Leave During Probation

- Annual leave entitlement accrues during the six-month probation period.
- Employees may use accrued annual leave after successfully completing probation.
- Exceptions during probation may be approved by the reporting manager and Human Resources in exceptional circumstances.

### 3.2 Annual Leave Carry-Forward

- Employees may carry forward a maximum of 5 unused annual leave days into the next calendar year.
- Any unused annual leave exceeding the 5-day carry-forward limit expires at the end of the leave year.
- Carried-forward leave must be used by March 31 of the following year.
- Unused carried-forward leave expires after March 31.

## 4. Sick Leave

- Eligible employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per calendar year.
- Sick leave may be used when an employee is unable to work due to illness or a medical condition.
- Employees must inform their reporting manager as soon as possible when taking unexpected sick leave.
- A medical certificate may be requested for sick leave exceeding 2 consecutive working days.
- Unused sick leave cannot be carried forward to the next calendar year.
- Sick leave cannot be converted into annual leave or exchanged for cash.

Employees on probation are also eligible to use sick leave.

## 5. Public Holidays

- The company provides 10 paid public holidays per calendar year.
- The official holiday calendar is published by Human Resources at the beginning of each calendar year.
- Public holidays are separate from annual leave and sick leave.
- Employees required to work on a public holiday must follow the applicable manager approval and compensatory-off process.

## 6. Leave Approval Process

Employees must follow these steps when requesting planned leave:

1. Submit a leave request through the company's HR system.
2. Select the leave category and requested dates.
3. Provide a reason if required by the HR system.
4. Wait for approval from the reporting manager.
5. Confirm the approved leave dates before making final arrangements.

Submitting a leave request does not automatically mean that the leave has been approved.

## 7. Emergency Leave and Unexpected Absence

- Employees facing emergencies must inform their reporting manager as soon as reasonably possible.
- If the HR system is unavailable, employees may notify their manager through an official communication channel.
- The leave request must be recorded in the HR system once the employee is able to do so.
- The manager may coordinate with Human Resources to determine the appropriate leave category.

## 8. Unpaid Leave

- Employees who have exhausted their applicable paid leave balances may request unpaid leave.
- Unpaid leave requires approval from both the reporting manager and Human Resources.
- Approval depends on business requirements and the circumstances of the request.
- Unpaid leave is not automatically granted.

## 9. Attendance Requirements

- Employees must record their attendance using the company's designated attendance system.
- Employees must follow their assigned working schedules.
- Repeated unapproved late arrivals or unexplained absences may result in disciplinary action.
- Any attendance record errors must be reported to the reporting manager or Human Resources.

## 10. Policy Administration

Human Resources is responsible for maintaining leave records and communicating policy updates.

For questions about leave balances, eligibility, or approvals, employees should contact Human Resources.
