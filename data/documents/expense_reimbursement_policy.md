# Expense Reimbursement Policy

**Company:** Nexora Technologies  
**Document Type:** Expense Reimbursement Policy  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines the rules and procedures for claiming reimbursement for eligible business-related expenses incurred by employees of Nexora Technologies.

The purpose is to ensure that business expenses are reasonable, properly documented, and approved before reimbursement.

## 2. General Eligibility

- Employees may claim reimbursement for expenses directly related to approved business activities.
- Expenses must be reasonable and necessary for business purposes.
- Personal expenses are not eligible for reimbursement.
- Employees must follow the approval process before incurring expenses whenever prior approval is required.
- All claims must include the required supporting documents.

## 3. Business Travel Approval

- Employees must obtain approval from their reporting manager before booking business travel.
- Travel requests must include the purpose, destination, expected duration, and estimated cost.
- Travel bookings must follow the company's approved travel arrangements.
- Any significant change to an approved travel plan must be communicated to the reporting manager.

## 4. Transportation Expenses

### 4.1 Air Travel

- Domestic business air travel must be booked in economy class.
- International business air travel must be booked in economy class unless an exception is approved by the department head.
- Employees should select reasonably priced tickets that meet business travel requirements.

### 4.2 Train Travel

- Domestic train travel may be booked in AC 2-tier class.
- Any exception requires prior approval from the department head.

### 4.3 Local Transportation

- Employees may claim reimbursement for reasonable local transportation expenses incurred during approved business activities.
- Eligible expenses include taxis, public transportation, and airport transfers.
- Claims must include receipts or other acceptable proof of payment.

## 5. Accommodation Expenses

- Hotel accommodation is reimbursable for approved business travel requiring an overnight stay.
- The standard accommodation limit is ₹4,000 per night for domestic business travel.
- Accommodation exceeding this limit requires prior approval from the department head.
- Employees must select reasonably priced accommodation suitable for business travel.

## 6. Meal Expenses

- Employees may claim reimbursement for meals during approved domestic business travel.
- The maximum meal reimbursement limit is ₹800 per employee per day.
- Alcoholic beverages and personal entertainment expenses are not reimbursable.
- Meal claims must be supported by itemized receipts.

## 7. Expense Documentation

Employees must provide the following information when submitting a reimbursement claim:

- Employee name and employee ID.
- Expense date.
- Expense category.
- Business purpose of the expense.
- Amount claimed.
- Supporting receipts or invoices.

Claims without sufficient supporting documentation may be rejected or returned for clarification.

## 8. Claim Submission Deadline

- Employees must submit reimbursement claims within 10 calendar days after completing the business activity or travel.
- Claims submitted after the deadline require an exception approved by Human Resources and the reporting manager.
- Employees should submit complete and accurate information to avoid processing delays.

## 9. Approval Authority

Expense claims are subject to the following approval limits:

- Claims up to ₹25,000 require approval from the employee's reporting manager.
- Claims exceeding ₹25,000 require approval from the department head.
- Expenses that exceed category-specific limits require the applicable additional approval, even if the total claim is below ₹25,000.

Employees cannot approve their own reimbursement claims.

## 10. Reimbursement Timeline

- Approved and complete reimbursement claims are normally processed within 15 business days after final approval.
- Incomplete claims may be returned to employees for additional information.
- The processing timeline begins again when the required missing information is submitted and the claim is approved.

## 11. Non-Reimbursable Expenses

The following expenses are not eligible for reimbursement:

- Personal shopping.
- Alcoholic beverages.
- Personal entertainment.
- Traffic fines and penalties.
- Expenses unrelated to approved business activities.
- Expenses already reimbursed by another source.
- Expenses that violate company policy.

## 12. Policy Violations

Submitting false, altered, or misleading expense claims may result in disciplinary action.

The company reserves the right to reject claims that do not comply with this policy.

## 13. Policy Administration

The Finance department is responsible for processing approved reimbursement claims.

Employees may contact Finance or Human Resources for clarification regarding expense eligibility, documentation, and reimbursement status.
